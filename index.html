FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Export any date range as a CSV from the Summary tab — your data is always yours to take with you.

Does it work offline?

The app installs to your home screen and opens instantly, but entries need a connection to save.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Pro is a monthly subscription with no contract — cancel anytime from your account settings, no questions asked.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Your entries and client data are encrypted in transit and at rest, and are never sold or shared.

Is it available on iPhone?

Not yet — Cruxy is currently available only on the Google Play Store. iOS is on the roadmap.
