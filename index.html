FAQ

Questions, answered.

What do I get on the Free plan?

The full app, with up to 2 active clients. Includes unlimited projects and tasks, invoices (3 emailed per month), CSV import and export, PDF client statements with your own logo, and US-based customer support.

When should I go Pro?

Go Pro when you're working with more than 2 active clients, or when you want more. You get all the Free features, plus unlimited active clients, unlimited emailed invoices, project budgets and tracking, monthly budget rollover, retainers, and invoices without the Cruxy footer, for $4.99 a month. Cancel anytime, no questions asked.

What's an active client?

Free includes 2 active clients. When you're done with a client, archive them and they stop counting. Archived clients keep all their history, read-only, and you can still export it. Or go Pro for unlimited active clients.

Can I send invoices from Cruxy?

Yes. Create a PDF invoice from your unbilled time, then email it to your client right from the app. Free includes 3 emailed invoices a month, and Pro is unlimited. You can always download the PDF and send it yourself.

Can I bill a retainer?

Yes, with Pro. Set a weekly, biweekly, monthly, or quarterly charge for a client, and each one shows up ready to add to your next invoice.

Can I switch from another time tracker?

Yes. Import a CSV time export from Clockify, Toggl, or Harvest and your entries come with you. If you have more than 2 clients on Free, the extra clients are imported as archived so you can choose which to keep active, or go Pro for unlimited active clients.

What happens if I change a client's rate?

Your past earnings don't change. Each entry keeps the rate it was logged at, so a new rate only applies to new entries. You can also switch any single entry to the current rate when you edit it.

Can I use it on more than one device?

Yes. Sign in on any device and your clients, projects, and entries are all there. A running timer stays on the device where you started it.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Export any date range as a CSV from the Entries or Summary tabs — your data is always yours to take with you. You can also delete your account anytime from Settings, which permanently deletes your data.

Does it work offline?

Yes, for logging time. If you're offline, your entries are saved on your device and sync automatically when you're back online. Editing or deleting entries needs a connection.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Pro is a monthly subscription with no contract — cancel anytime from your account settings, no questions asked.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Your entries and client data are encrypted in transit and at rest, and are never sold or shared.

Is it available on iPhone?

Yes, on the web. Open get.cruxy.io in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen, and Cruxy opens like an app. An App Store version is coming soon.
