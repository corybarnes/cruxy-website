Privacy Policy

Last updated October 8, 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, password (stored encrypted), workspace name.
  • Billing information: for paid workspaces, your subscription is processed by Stripe; we do not store your card number — Stripe handles that directly.
  • Workspace data: time entries, clients, tasks, and related notes that you or your teammates create.
  • Usage and device information: IP address, browser type, and basic interaction data needed to operate and secure the service.
  • Website contact form: if you write to us from cruxy.io, we receive your name, email address, company name and message by email. If you tick the box, we also add you to our marketing list. Either way, your name, email and company are saved to our email system (Klaviyo).
  • Marketing preferences: whether you opted in to product tips and updates when you signed up. Beyond the key milestones and alerts described under Klaviyo below, your workspace data is not sent to our marketing system.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to operate the service — running the timer, generating reports, managing workspace membership and billing, authenticating your account, and providing customer support. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. How We Share Information

We share information only with the service providers who help us run Cruxy:

  • Stripe — payment processing for paid subscriptions
  • Supabase — database hosting and authentication
  • Vercel — application hosting
  • Cloudflare — bot/spam protection on sign-up
  • Resend — delivery of account emails, of invoices you choose to email to your clients, and of payment reminders you turn on for a client (we send these to the billing email you enter for that client, and Resend tells us whether each one was delivered or bounced)
  • Google Analytics — anonymous usage statistics: which pages are visited and which key steps (such as signing up or starting an upgrade) are completed. It does not receive your workspace data, such as client names, hours or amounts
  • Klaviyo — product emails and usage insights: your name, email, workspace name, plan, simple usage counts, and details of key milestones (such as the names of your first client and project, or the number and total of your first invoice). We use this to understand how Cruxy is used and to send relevant product emails. If you keep Overdue Invoice Alerts on (Settings), we also send the client name, invoice number, total and due date of your invoices that are past due, so we can email you about them; you can turn these alerts off at any time. Promotional emails are sent only if you opted in, and you can unsubscribe at any time

4. Workspace Data

Time entries, clients, and tasks you create belong to your workspace. Workspace owners and admins control member access and can see workspace activity. If you leave a workspace, your workspace data remains with that workspace.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes. You can export or delete your workspace data at any time from account settings.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We recommend using a strong, unique password for your account.

8. Children's Data

The service is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the age of majority in your jurisdiction).

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We’ll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Contact us at support@cruxy.io.
