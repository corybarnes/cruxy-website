# Billing

> Cruxy Pro is $4.99 a month. On the web, payments are handled by Stripe. In the Android app, they go through Google Play.

Source: https://cruxy.io/support/billing/ (updated 2026-10-14)

## Upgrade to Pro

1. Open **Settings > Profile** and tap **Go Pro**. You can also tap **Go Pro** when Cruxy offers it, for example when you reach a limit.
2. Pay on the checkout page. On the web that is Stripe. In the Android app it is Google Play.
3. When you return to Cruxy, you're on Pro straight away.


Pro is billed monthly. There's no contract.

## Where you manage your plan

It depends on where you paid. Open **Settings > Profile** and look at the Plan row:
- **Manage Subscription** means you paid on the web with Stripe. Tap it to open Stripe's billing page.
- **Managed in Google Play** means you subscribed in the Android app. Manage it in the Google Play app: tap your profile picture, then **Payments & subscriptions**, then **Subscriptions**, then Cruxy.

## Receipts and invoices

If you paid with Stripe, you can see past payments and download receipts from the billing page behind **Manage Subscription**. If you paid through Google Play, Google emails your receipts and lists them under Payments & subscriptions. If you need a receipt in a particular form, email [support@cruxy.io](mailto:support@cruxy.io).

## Update your card

With Stripe, tap **Manage Subscription** and update your card on the billing page. With Google Play, change your payment method in the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions.

## Cancel Pro

Cancel where you pay: **Manage Subscription** for Stripe, or Subscriptions in the Google Play app. You can cancel at any time. In the last week of your plan Cruxy shows a banner with the date it ends. If you paid with Stripe, a **Keep Pro** button lets you change your mind. After that your workspace moves to Free. See [If Pro ends](https://cruxy.io/support/free-vs-pro/#pro-ends).

## Refunds

Fees are non-refundable except as required by law. If you were charged by mistake, email [support@cruxy.io](mailto:support@cruxy.io) and we'll look into it. If you paid through Google Play, Google handles refund requests for those purchases.

Deleting your account cancels a Stripe subscription straight away and doesn't refund the current period. A Google Play subscription must be cancelled in Google Play.
