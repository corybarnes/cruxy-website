# Billing

> Cruxy Pro is $4.99 a month. Payments are handled by Stripe.

Source: https://cruxy.io/support/billing/ (updated 2026-10-14)

## Upgrade to Pro

1. Open **Settings > Profile** and tap **Go Pro**. You can also tap **Go Pro** when Cruxy offers it, for example when you reach a limit.
2. Enter your payment details on the Stripe checkout page.
3. When you return to Cruxy, you're on Pro straight away.


Pro is billed monthly. There's no contract.

## Receipts and invoices

Stripe handles your payments and billing history. You can see past payments, and download receipts, from the billing page behind **Manage Subscription**. If you need a receipt in a particular form, email [support@cruxy.io](mailto:support@cruxy.io).

## Manage your subscription or update your card

Open **Settings > Profile** and tap **Manage Subscription**. This opens Stripe's billing page, where you can update your card, see your payments, or cancel.

## Cancel Pro

Open **Settings > Profile**, tap **Manage Subscription**, and cancel there. You can cancel at any time. In the last week of your plan Cruxy shows a banner with the date it ends and a **Keep Pro** button if you change your mind. After that your workspace moves to Free. See [If Pro ends](https://cruxy.io/support/free-vs-pro/#pro-ends).

## Refunds

Fees are non-refundable except as required by law. If you were charged by mistake, email [support@cruxy.io](mailto:support@cruxy.io) and we'll look into it.

If you subscribed through Google Play in the Android app, Google Play handles the refund under its refund policy.

Deleting your account cancels Pro straight away and doesn't refund the current period.
